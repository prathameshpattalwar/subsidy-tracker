Facing Issues?

Finding and Tracking government subsidies is currently confusing and fragmented for everyday citizens.

  • Scattered Portals: Welfare schemes are spread across dozens of websites, making eligibility hard to find.
  • Zero Tracking Visibility : Citizens cannot easily see where their Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) payment is stuck.
  • Slow Issue Resolution: Reporting missing or delayed funds requires visiting offices and dealing with complex paperwork.

Why Use Subsidy Tracker?

The Subsidy Tracker is a centralized platform for citizens to access and monitor their direct benefits transfers (DBT) and subsidy applications.

Our mission: Ensure efficiency and accountability in benefit delivery.

Citizens need a single, transparent digital bridge between government welfare programs and their bank accounts.

  • Centralized Discovery: One portal to find all eligible state and central schemes in seconds.
  • Real-Time Tracking: Step-by-step updates from application approval to bank deposit.
  • Direct Accountability: A fast online system to report and fix delayed payments without middlemen.
